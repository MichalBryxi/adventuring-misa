Hi, I'm Míša. I'm Czech and grew up in Pilsen, then lived in Belfast, Edinburgh and Inverness, and now I live in [Interlaken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interlaken), between the lakes, with the Swiss Alps on the doorstep. This blog is where I write up my adventures in the mountains and things around.

## Outdoors

- **Trail running.** Races up to 100 km: Baroko 100, Matterhorn Ultraks Extreme, E51, Ben Nevis Ultra, the [Ring of Steall](/2019-09-21-salomon-ring-of-steall-skyrace), the [Thieves Road](/2019-08-24-thieves-road-race), the [Tromsø Skyrace](/2019-08-03-tromso-skyrace-tromsdalstinden), the Niesen Treppenlauf and the Vertical Kilometre du Mont Blanc. All the race reports are under [race](/tag/race).
- **Hiking and scrambling.** From [Munros](/tag/munro) in Scotland to [T6 routes](/2026-11-15-katzenwegli-t6) in the Bernese Oberland.
- **Climbing.** A hobby climber up to 6b+, mostly sport, plus bouldering, multi-pitch and trad, and via ferratas up to D. I've introduced plenty of people to climbing and bouldering, and did the SAC course for setting boulder routes.
- **Paragliding.** Solo pilot (IPPI 4), flying in Switzerland, around Central Europe and in Nepal.
- **Skiing and ski touring.** I taught myself to ski on the pistes a few years ago, and now I'm heading off them, ski touring for the freedom of the open Alps.
- **Biking.** Bikepacking from Czechia to the sea, from Havana to the westernmost point of Cuba, and along part of Ireland's Wild Atlantic Way. I'm an active [Warmshowers](https://www.warmshowers.org) host, so if you're cycling through Interlaken, get in touch.
- **Guiding.** I've guided e-bike tours around Interlaken, for Loop Tours in 2024 and [Flying Wheels](https://www.flyingwheels.ch) in 2025.

## Computers

By day I'm a full-stack web developer, mostly with [Ember.js](https://emberjs.com). I've built front ends at Puppet and CrowdStrike, and for eleven years I've run [beauty-pay.cz](https://beauty-pay.cz), a booking and management app for beauty salons. At [EmberFest 2026](https://emberfest.eu) in Prague I gave a talk about it: [4,207 Days of Uptime: Evolving an Ember App Without a Rewrite](https://github.com/MichalBryxi/EmberFest-2026-Prague-4207-Days-of-Uptime).

Much of my code ends up outdoors:

- **[winds.mobi](https://winds.mobi)**, real-time wind from weather stations across the Alps and beyond, which paragliders check before they fly. I build its [new web client](https://github.com/winds-mobi/winds-mobi-client-web).
- **[Forkcast](https://forkcast.vast.earth)**, a [vast.earth](https://vast.earth) project that shows what the weather usually does along a bikepacking route, day by day, so you can plan a long trip months ahead.

Smaller ones too: [Magic Pass places as GPX](/2023-05-10-magic-pass-places-as-gpx), a [Skytraxx vario to CSV export](https://gitlab.com/michal-bryxi/open-source/vario-skytraxx-to-csv) for my paragliding flights, and this blog itself, which is [open source](https://github.com/MichalBryxi/adventuring-misa).

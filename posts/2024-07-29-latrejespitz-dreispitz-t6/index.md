*A cute little one: a massive ascent for a 200 m traverse.*

## The story

I started straight from the village of Kiental. No cable car this time, so all 1700 m of ascent is on the legs.

The first part is a long, gentle walk up the valley on alp roads and paths, with the view opening up behind me with every bend. Then the path runs out and it's **steep grass** all the way up to the ridge.

On the ridge I first went north, for an out-and-back to **First**. Not on the T6 itself, but a grassy summit with **Lake Thun** right below, which was worth the detour.

Back south, the ridge turns into **grey limestone towers**: Latrejespitz. This is the T6 bit, and it's short, maybe 200 m. Slabs, a bit of exposure, and a couple of **fixed ropes**.

I used the ropes, though I wasn't sure how solid they were: the **sheath was worn** and damaged in places. Looking back, it was very unlikely they would fail. It's definitely possible to cross without them, but they're a good safety feature, and it was very nice and reassuring to have them there.

From Latrejespitz it's an easy walk up to the cross on **Dreispitz**, where I had a long break in the sun.

The way down goes along the ridge to the west, past an alp hut to **Renggpass**, and from there down the valley back to Kiental.

It's a lot of up for a very short bit of fun, but it was a fun day out.

*Sun, exposure, an elevated heart rate and gorgeous views. I did not feel so happy in months.*

## The story

On 1 June 2025 a couple of friends **took me along** on the Niesengrat, the long ridge that runs south from the Niesen. ~22 km, ~2100 m up, and ample beautiful scrambles.

### Up the north ridge

We started in **Wimmis**, past a farm with a sign warning about the animals, and went straight up the **north ridge** instead of the usual path. Steep green meadows first, with **Lake Thun** opening up below us more with every step.

At the top is **Niesen Kulm**, with the funicular station, the terrace and the whole Bernese Oberland lined up behind it. A year earlier I had run up the Niesen's stairs in the stair race. This time the way up was a lot more fun.

### Along the chain

From the top the real day starts, and I like this ridge because it **starts with the crux**. And the crux is very well defined: about **3 m of vertical rock** with via ferrata steps and a **rope**, on very solid rock. High consequences, but very low risk, and very reversible if you decide to turn back.

Past that the difficulty flows between **T4 and T5**, and the ridge goes on and on: grassy crests with a **steep drop** on one side, then **rock steps** where you put your hands down and scramble.

That makes it one of the T6s I'd call **beginner friendly**, if you want to get into T6.

In between: a **narrow rock ridge** with Lake Thun far below, **pasque flowers** all over the grass, and in early June still **patches of snow** on the slopes. The highest point is the **Fromberghore**, 2394 m.

### Down to Wiler

After the Fromberghore we dropped down to **Wiler** near Oey in the Diemtigtal. The chain goes on to the Mäggisserehore, but we didn't get there that day, so I came back for it a year later: Mäggisserehore, Schmelihore, Honiese.

This was the day that got me back to collecting T6s.

## Learnings

- [+] **Go up the north ridge**
  It turns the Niesen from a funicular summit into the first good part of the day.
- [i] **Or take the funicular up**
  The Niesen funicular takes you to the start of the ridge, so you meet the crux with fresh legs and a clear head.
- [i] **Early June is early**
  Flowers out, but still snow patches on the ridge. Check the shady side before you go.
- [*] **The company**
  Friends who knew the way, and a day of scrambling in the sun.

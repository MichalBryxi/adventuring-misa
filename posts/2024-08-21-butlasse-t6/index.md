*So far the nicest scramble around here.*

## The story

From Griesalp it's a long walk up the valley along the stream, past the alps and a few cows, to **Sefinenfurgge**. The pass has a bench, which was the last comfortable seat of the day.

From the pass the **NW ridge** goes straight up, all of it in and out of the clouds. Every now and then the clouds opened and showed the **snowy peaks** all around, with a few snow patches still on the ridge in late August.

The first half, from the saddle up, is a **scree field** where you constantly knock your ankles on the loose rocks. Whoever drew the route through it, I'm not a fan. Halfway up it turns into **solid-ish rock**, and the scrambling from there is very easy for a T6.

The SAC route is an out-and-back from Griesalp. I came back down the ridge to Sefinenfurgge and went over to the other side instead: down the **ladders** below the pass, past the **Rotstockhütte** and on to Gimmelwald. So much nicer than going back the same way.

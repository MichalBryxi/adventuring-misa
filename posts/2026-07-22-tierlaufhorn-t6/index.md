*My nemesis from 2024.*

## The story

### The first attempt

On 2 September 2024 I went up from the Diemtigtal on my own. On the way up, a yellow trail arrow had a note taped on it: *"Dies ist ein Wanderwegpfeil, aber Du suchst ein Wanderwegposten! Laufe und suche weiter!"* (This is a trail arrow, but you're looking for a trail post! Keep running and keep looking!)

I got to about **60 m below the summit**, only around 200 m away from it. But the **technical difficulty**, the **wet rock** and a **storm coming in** all said nope. It's sad knowing the next checkmark is that close, but I turned around.

### Back, two years later

On 22 July 2026 I came back, this time not alone and in perfect weather.

The **first bit** is the **crux**: very intimidating, moderately chossy, and I have no idea how I did it in 2024. **Commit to it or turn around**, the rest is easier. At the top of it there are a couple of bolts and pitons with **fixed ropes**, and people seem to leave a bit more material every year. It doesn't help all the way, but it helps.

Then a grassy slope and a few easy rocky steps lead up to a ridge that feels like the top. It isn't: the real peak sits off to the right, a big round block that looks **impossible** from a distance. We followed the ridge over to it on its left side, a bit chossy but never dangerous, and once below the block, the way up turns out to be quite intuitive. Left of the block runs a **couloir**, not entirely stable but with big solid rocks.

Make every move a careful one. A fall there means a helicopter ride.

At the top there's even a **summit book**. So beautiful.

On the way down through the couloir, a **sling** made the down climbing much easier. At the fixed ropes I left a piece of my cord to make them a bit longer and sturdier, so if you see a **white and green** one, that was me.

## Learnings

- [+] **Turned around in 2024**
  Wet rock, real difficulty and a storm coming in. The summit waited two years for me.
- [+] **The sling**
  Wrapped around a big, solidly stuck rock, it's a great aid for down climbing. Getting it back is the annoying part.
- [+] **Next time, cord**
  Unlike the sling, cord can stay behind for whoever comes next.

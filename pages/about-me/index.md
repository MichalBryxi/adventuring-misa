Hi, I'm Míša. I'm Czech and grew up in Pilsen, then lived in Belfast, Edinburgh and Inverness, and now I live in [Interlaken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interlaken), between the lakes, with the Swiss Alps on the doorstep. This blog is where I write up my adventures in the mountains and things around.

## Outdoors

### 🏃 Trail running

Races up to 100 km: Baroko 100, Matterhorn Ultraks Extreme, E51, Ben Nevis Ultra, the [Ring of Steall](../../posts/2019-09-21-salomon-ring-of-steall-skyrace/index.md), the [Thieves Road](../../posts/2019-08-24-thieves-road-race/index.md), the [Tromsø Skyrace](../../posts/2019-08-03-tromso-skyrace-tromsdalstinden/index.md), the Niesen Treppenlauf and the Vertical Kilometre du Mont Blanc. All the race reports are under [race](/?tags=race).

### 🥾 Hiking and scrambling

From [Munros](/?tags=munro) in Scotland to [T6 routes](../../posts/2024-06-25-katzenwegli-t6/index.md) in the Bernese Oberland.

### 🧗 Climbing

A hobby climber up to 6b+, mostly sport, plus bouldering, multi-pitch and trad, and via ferratas up to D. I've introduced plenty of people to climbing and bouldering, and did the SAC course for setting boulder routes.

### 🪂 Paragliding

Solo pilot (IPPI 4), flying in Switzerland, around Central Europe and in Nepal.

### ⛷️ Skiing and ski touring

I taught myself to ski on the pistes a few years ago, and now I'm heading off them, ski touring for the freedom of the open Alps.

### 🚴 Biking

Bikepacking from Czechia to the sea, from Havana to the westernmost point of Cuba, and along part of Ireland's Wild Atlantic Way. I'm an active [Warmshowers](https://www.warmshowers.org) host, so if you're cycling through Interlaken, get in touch.

### 🚵 Guiding

I've guided e-bike tours around Interlaken, for Loop Tours in 2024 and [Flying Wheels](https://www.flyingwheels.ch) in 2025.

## Vegan

### 🫕 Vegan fondue

Fondue is a Swiss mountain classic, and it works just as well without the cheese. I help put on vegan fondue evenings in the Bernese Oberland, with skiing first at the [Mountain Hostel](https://www.mountainhostel.com) in Gimmelwald in January 2026, and next up the Heimwehfluh above Interlaken. Supported by [New Roots](https://newroots.ch) and [Tier im Fokus](https://www.tier-im-fokus.ch).

## Computers

### 💇 Beauty salons

[beauty-pay.cz](https://beauty-pay.cz), a booking and management app for beauty salons, which I've run for eleven years.

### 🌬️ Paragliding weather

[winds.mobi](https://winds.mobi) shows real-time wind from weather stations across the Alps and beyond, and paragliders check it before they fly. I build its [new web client](https://github.com/winds-mobi/winds-mobi-client-web).

### 🌦️ Bikepacking weather

[Forkcast](https://forkcast.vast.earth) shows what the weather usually does along a bike route, day by day, so you can plan a long trip months ahead.

### 🗺️ Magic Pass on the map

[Every Magic Pass place as a GPX](../../posts/2023-05-10-magic-pass-places-as-gpx/index.md), so you can see on a map where the pass is valid.

### 📈 Paragliding flight stats

[Vario Skytraxx to CSV](https://gitlab.com/michal-bryxi/open-source/vario-skytraxx-to-csv) exports the statistics of paraglider and hang glider flights from a Skytraxx 2.0 vario.

### 🧩 [Rental shop check-in](https://github.com/MichalBryxi/approom-customiser)

[AppRoom Customiser](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/approom-customiser/nknnolnmhnmikpopfakfpmoggccciefb), a Chrome extension that adds workflow helpers to the App-Room ERP: print buttons, barcode check-in and registration automation.

### 📅 Poll to calendar

[nuudel to ICS](https://nuudel-to-ics.netlify.app) turns a nuudel "Termin finden" poll into calendar files you can import anywhere.

### 🎵 Song flipbook

[Spotify Flipbook](https://spotify-flipbook.netlify.app) makes a printable flipbook from a Spotify playlist.

### 📝 [This blog](https://github.com/MichalBryxi/adventuring-misa)

The website you're reading: a folder of Markdown on GitHub, built with [cowtent](https://github.com/vast-ch/cowtent-core), a blog platform made with Ember.
